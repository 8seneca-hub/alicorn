## The one sequencing judgment that matters

**SK1 is technically unblocked and should still wait.**

Its dependencies (WF4, GP2, CW1) are all Done, so nothing stops it mechanically. But SK1 is *level 3
automatic retirement and demotion* — gates that retire themselves on track record. ARCHITECTURE §7 and
CLAUDE.md are explicit: autonomy is unlocked by evidence, and evidence only accumulates by running
gated. The thing that produces that evidence is **GP3**, which records whether the human agreed with
what the policy would have decided. GP3 has not shipped.

Shipping SK1 first would retire gates against a track record nobody has checked against human judgement
— which is precisely the "silent quality drift" the ROADMAP names as a top risk. GP3 first, then let it
run, then SK1.
