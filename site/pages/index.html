The problem

Your agents got fast. Your team got slower.

Developers using AI complete 21% more tasks and merge 98% more pull requests — and then everything jams. Review time is up 441%. Agentic PRs sit 5.3× longer before anyone picks them up. Trust in the output has fallen to 29%.

The reason is specific, and no better model fixes it: a reviewer receives a finished diff with no implementation journey and no decision trail. They reconstruct intent from the ticket and the code. So they stay in the loop for everything — approving plans, approving edits, approving commands — because they cannot tell what happened when they weren't watching.

The autonomy rule

Three gates in eight steps — and not the ones you'd guess.

A step keeps its human gate when it is neither machine-verifiable nor cheaply reversible, or when the member has no track record on it yet. Everything else runs and reports.
