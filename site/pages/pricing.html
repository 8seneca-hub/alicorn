Common questions

Do I pay for agent usage?

Not to us. Agents run on your machine under your own subscription. We never see a model key.

Is there a free tier?

Yes — up to three builder seats, one project, and the full decision trail. No card required.

Can we self-host on Team?

Self-hosting is available on every plan. Enterprise adds compliance evidence, custom retention and an SLA.

What happens if we leave?

Export the full decision trail as signed JSON at any time. It is your audit record, not ours.
